INTERVIEW BANK

What can be asked?

HR — Common Questions

  1. Tell me about yourself.
  2. Why should we hire you?
  3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
  4. Why do you want this role/company?
  5. Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?
  6. Tell us about a project you are proud of.
  7. Describe a challenge and how you solved it.
  8. Are you comfortable working in a team?

Electrical — Technical

  1. State and explain Kirchhoff's laws.
  2. Difference between AC and DC machines.
  3. Explain transformer working and losses.
  4. What is slip in an induction motor?
  5. Explain torque-slip characteristics.
  6. What is power factor and how can it be improved?
  7. Difference between MCB, MCCB and relay.
  8. Explain SCR, MOSFET and IGBT.
  9. What is feedback in a control system?
  10. Explain transmission-line losses.

Aptitude & Communication

  1. Percentages and profit/loss.
  2. Ratio, proportion and averages.
  3. Time, speed and distance.
  4. Probability and basic statistics.
  5. Logical reasoning and series.
  6. Reading comprehension and grammar.
  7. Explain your project in 60 seconds.
  8. Give a short technical introduction.

Resume Checklist

  • Keep a student resume concise.
  • Put skills you can actually demonstrate.
  • Add projects with your contribution and outcome.
  • Include internships/certificates only when genuine.
  • Check spelling, links and contact details.
  • Tailor the resume to the role.
